Small places rarely get the kind of careful digital portrait that larger towns take for granted. This site treats Hofstetten as a place worth looking at closely, with restrained design, strong photography, and claims tied back to their sources.

### What it does

- Connects the village's history with its landscape, St. Anna chapel, and present-day community life.
- Documents sources and image licences directly instead of leaving provenance in a separate research folder.
- Delivers a fast multi-page site with responsive images, accessible structure, and almost no browser-side JavaScript.

### Why it matters

Local history becomes more useful when it is specific, verifiable, and pleasant to explore. The result is less like a tourism template and more like a small, durable book about one place.
