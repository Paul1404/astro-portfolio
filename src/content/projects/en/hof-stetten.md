I built a website about Hofstetten in Lower Franconia, covering its landscape, local history, St. Anna chapel and present-day village life. Historical information is linked to its sources, and image credits appear alongside the content.

### Design and implementation

The multi-page Astro website combines text with large photographs. Responsive image sizes, an accessible page structure and little JavaScript keep the pages readable on mobile devices.

Photograph: [Tilman2007, St. Anna in Hofstetten](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hofstetten_27_K%C3%B6nigsberg_in_Bayern_20201129_001.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The website preview is cropped and compressed.
